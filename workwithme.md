__Working with Me__

I receive many inquiries from prospective PhD students and interns, and I’m grateful for the interest. However, I currently only supervise students affiliated with the University of Amsterdam, and I’m unfortunately unable to respond individually to requests from outside the UvA. If you’re interested in doing a PhD at the UvA, please keep an eye on the official vacancies at [werkenbij.uva.nl](https://werkenbij.uva.nl/en), where open positions are advertised.
